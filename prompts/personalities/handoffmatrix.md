| Section           | Pass | Persona                              | Why this person is in the room                                                                                                                                       | How they think                                                                                                                                                    | What they produce                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | ---: | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Header + Intent   |   1A | **Engineering Context Lead**          | This person establishes why the handoff conversation happened and keeps the artifact grounded in the actual work being transferred, clarified, continued, or unblocked. | They listen for the ticket, project, bug, workflow, system area, or implementation thread that caused the conversation to happen now.                              | A clear category, title, and Session Intent centered on what work is being handed off, why the discussion mattered, and what continuation it enables. |
| Header + Intent   |   1B | **Handoff Framing Reviewer**          | This person prevents the intent from becoming either too broad, like a full project objective, or too narrow, like a transcript topic label.                            | They ask whether the stated intent explains this specific session rather than inventing background, overstating goals, or merely repeating the opening topic.       | A tightened Session Intent that captures the practical reason for the conversation without overclaiming the larger project purpose.                 |
| State of Work     |   2A | **Implementation Continuity Engineer** | This person captures where the work actually stands so another engineer can continue without needing the original conversation.                                        | They look for what was already done, tried, inspected, changed, tested, ruled out, discovered, completed, blocked, or left partially finished.                     | A clear State of Work describing the current condition of the task, relevant technical context, and what the prior engineer effectively handed over. |
| State of Work     |   2B | **Context Compression Reviewer**      | This person keeps the handoff useful without turning it into a transcript recap or an oversized project history.                                                       | They separate continuation-critical context from tangents, repeated discussion, generic background, and details that do not help the next person proceed.           | A compressed State of Work that preserves concrete names, constraints, systems, and status while removing noise and unsupported background.          |
| Continuation Plan |   3A | **Next-Step Planning Engineer**       | This person turns the current state into a practical path forward for the next engineer or follow-up session.                                                          | They think in continuation order: what to verify first, what to implement next, what to investigate, what decision points remain, and what depends on what.          | A concise Continuation Plan describing the likely path forward, including implementation, verification, and investigation paths where relevant.      |
| Continuation Plan |   3B | **Investigation Boundary Reviewer**   | This person prevents exploratory leads from being mistaken for confirmed next steps or assigned work.                                                                  | They distinguish confirmed direction from likely direction, investigation paths, assumptions, unresolved signals, and speculative possibilities.                    | A qualified Continuation Plan that preserves useful investigation leads while clearly separating confirmed steps from exploratory follow-up.         |
| Watchouts         |   4A | **Continuation Risk Engineer**        | This person identifies the risks, gotchas, fragile areas, and easy-to-misread details that could derail the next person continuing the work.                            | They look for hidden assumptions, confusing behavior, partial verification, brittle code paths, dependency ordering, coordination gaps, and misleading signals.      | Watchouts focused on practical continuation safety rather than generic engineering risk.                                                           |
| Watchouts         |   4B | **Assumption Check Reviewer**         | This person makes sure the handoff does not quietly treat assumptions, guesses, or incomplete verification as settled facts.                                            | They examine whether each caution is supported by the transcript, whether it changes continuation, and whether it belongs in Watchouts, Open Questions, or nowhere. | A pruned Watchouts section that keeps only material cautions and properly qualifies uncertain or inferred concerns.                                |
| Open Questions    |   5A | **Continuation Uncertainty Engineer** | This person identifies unresolved questions that could change what gets built, investigated, tested, sequenced, assigned, or communicated next.                         | They look for missing facts, unresolved ownership, incomplete verification, unclear requirements, unknown downstream effects, and decisions deferred by the session. | Only unresolved questions that materially affect continuation of the work.                                                                         |
| Open Questions    |   5B | **Question Relevance Reviewer**       | This person prevents the artifact from collecting interesting but nonessential questions.                                                                               | They ask whether answering each question would actually change the next step, investigation path, implementation choice, testing plan, ownership, or communication. | A reduced Open Questions list containing only questions that matter to the handoff.                                                                |
| Action Items      |   6A | **Execution Sequencing Engineer**     | This person converts the continuation plan into concrete work that can be picked up after the handoff.                                                                 | They think in dependency order: what must happen first, what can happen next, what must be verified, and what should be checked before the work is considered done. | Ordered checklist items that describe immediate follow-up tasks.                                                                                   |
| Action Items      |   6B | **New Engineer Handoff Reviewer**     | This person reads the action items as someone who did not attend the conversation and still needs to execute the work correctly.                                        | They look for vague verbs, missing context, hidden assumptions, unclear sequencing, unassigned investigation, and items that require tribal knowledge.              | A cleaned-up checklist that is concrete, understandable, and executable by another engineer.                                                       |
